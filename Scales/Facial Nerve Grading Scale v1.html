Regional Score Definitions

  • 1 - Normal: Symmetric function, identical to the contralateral side.
  • 2 - Mild Dysfunction: Obvious movement on careful inspection, slight asymmetry.
  • 3 - Moderate Dysfunction: Obvious asymmetry, but some movement is present.
  • 4 - Severe Dysfunction: Only a flicker of movement is detectable.
  • 5 - Paralysis: No movement.
